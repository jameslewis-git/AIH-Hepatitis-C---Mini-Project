About This Project

Hepatitis C is a blood-borne viral infection that progressively damages the liver, advancing through distinct clinical stages — from a healthy baseline to Hepatitis, Fibrosis, and ultimately Cirrhosis.

Traditional staging relies on invasive liver biopsy. This application offers a non-invasive alternative using machine learning to predict disease stage from 12 routine laboratory markers:

Age, Sex, Albumin (ALB), Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP), ALT, AST, Bilirubin (BIL), Cholinesterase (CHE), Cholesterol (CHOL), Creatinine (CREA), GGT, Total Protein (PROT)

Disease Stages Detected
  • Blood Donor — Healthy
  • Suspect — Borderline
  • Hepatitis — Active infection
  • Fibrosis — Early scarring
  • Cirrhosis — Advanced damage
⚠️ Academic Demonstration Only
This tool is not a certified medical device and must never be used for clinical diagnosis.
