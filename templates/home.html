About the Project

Non-Invasive Liver Staging via AI

Hepatitis C progressively damages the liver through distinct clinical stages. Traditional staging requires an invasive liver biopsy. This model offers a non-invasive alternative using XGBoost trained on the UCI HCV dataset.

The algorithm analyzes 12 routine blood panel markers simultaneously to detect patterns that correlate with each disease stage, returning a probability distribution across all 5 classes.

Biomarkers: Age · Sex · ALB · ALP · ALT · AST · BIL · CHE · CHOL · CREA · GGT · PROT
