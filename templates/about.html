Architecture

Model Architecture

The pipeline trains both Random Forest and XGBoost classifiers on the UCI HCV dataset. The model achieving the highest macro-F1 score is automatically selected and serialized to disk — ensuring the best performer is always deployed without manual intervention.

The full bundle saved to model/hcv_model.pkl includes the fitted imputer, scaler, trained classifier, feature names, and class labels — enabling fully reproducible inference.
